<h2 align="center">✨ Features</h2>

<p align="center">

  &nbsp; • &nbsp;
  📕 TXT → EPUB
  &nbsp; • &nbsp;
  📗 EPUB → TXT
</p>

<h2 align="center">📄 Novel Text Formatter</h2>

<p align="center">
  Clean and format raw novel text with advanced processing options.
</p>

<p align="center">
  ✂️ Chapter splitting &nbsp; • &nbsp;
  ↩️ Intelligent line breaks &nbsp; • &nbsp;
  📝 Paragraph formatting<br>
  🔤 Simplified ↔ Traditional Chinese &nbsp; • &nbsp;
  🧹 Duplicate-line removal<br>
  🚫 Website and spam-text filtering
</p>

<h2 align="center">📕 TXT → EPUB</h2>

<p align="center">
  Convert TXT novel files into properly structured EPUB ebooks.
</p>

<h2 align="center">📗 EPUB → TXT</h2>

<p align="center">
  Convert EPUB ebooks back into TXT while preserving chapter order and text structure.
</p>

<h2 align="center">🔒 Privacy</h2>

<p align="center">
  NovelCraft follows a local-first approach whenever possible.
  Files can be processed directly in your browser without unnecessary uploads.
</p>
